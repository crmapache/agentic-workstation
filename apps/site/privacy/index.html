Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026.

Agentic Workstation is a desktop app for macOS and Windows. It runs on your computer and works with the files and apps you give it. This policy explains where your data goes when you use the app and this website.

Your code and conversations

Conversations with the AI are run by the AI tool installed on your computer: Claude Code, Codex, the Antigravity CLI, Kimi Code or OpenCode. That tool sends your messages, the files it reads and the results of its commands to its AI provider, for example Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for Codex, Google for Antigravity or Moonshot AI for Kimi, under that provider's terms. Conversation history is kept by that tool on your computer. You sign in to Antigravity and Kimi with that tool itself: the app shows the sign-in page and passes the code you paste to the tool, and it never sees your password or tokens.

The app does not send your code, files or conversations to the developer. Statistics, local file history and settings are stored only on your computer.

Task tracker and cloud sync

The task tracker keeps a board for each of your projects. The boards are stored on your computer and synced to the developer's cloud at agentic-workstation-cloud.mzpizote.com, so another computer with the same account key sees them too. Before anything leaves your computer, the app encrypts it with your account key. The cloud stores only that ciphertext, a random id and a version number for each record, and it has no key to read them.

The account key is made on your computer the first time you open the tracker and is kept in your system keychain. The app signs every request with a key derived from it, and the cloud knows your account only by the public part of that signing key. The cloud sees the IP address of each request and does not keep it in its database. Without the account key your boards cannot be read or restored, by you or by the developer. You can turn sync off in the tracker; the boards then stay only on your computer.

Open models

The open models panel runs OpenCode with models from the connections you set up. Ollama and LM Studio are found on your computer, and their models stay there. When you add OpenRouter or another address, the app sends your key to that address to check it and to list the models, and it asks OpenRouter how much of the key's credit is used. Your conversations then go to the model service you picked.

Keys of these connections are stored encrypted on your computer. OpenCode does not get them: its requests to the model service pass through the app on your computer, which adds the key. The key is not sent anywhere else.

Apps you connect

Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Slack, Jira and Confluence, Todoist and other MCP servers are reached only after you connect them in the app. Requests go directly from your computer to each service. Google accounts sign in through a Google app that you create yourself.

  • Sign-in tokens and keys are stored on your computer, encrypted with the system's key storage: the Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows.
  • Whatever the AI reads from a connected app becomes part of the conversation and is sent to your AI provider.
  • Actions that send something to other people, such as sending mail or inviting guests, stay off until you turn them on.
  • Removing a connector deletes its tokens from your computer.

Browser and Computer Use

The Browser connector works through an extension in your Chrome that talks only to the app on your computer. Pages the AI opens and reads become part of the conversation.

Computer Use takes screenshots of your screen and sends them to your AI provider as part of the conversation. It does this only while a conversation is using it, and Pause and Stop stay on screen the whole time it works.

Remote Control

Remote Control is off until you pair a phone. Then the app and your phone connect through the developer's relay at agentic-workstation-relay.mzpizote.com. Everything between them is end-to-end encrypted, and the relay has no keys to read it. The relay sees device addresses made from random keys, the size and time of each message and the IP address of each connection. It stores nothing on disk, and its logs are deleted after 7 days. Notifications reach your phone through its browser's push service, encrypted so that only your phone can read them. The details are on the relay's privacy page.

Extensions and the extension store

Extensions run on your computer inside a sandbox. Each one can use only what you allow it: files of your open projects, your AI chats, the sites and programs listed in it, the clipboard. An extension you allow to use the network can send data to the sites it lists. That traffic goes from your computer to those sites, not through the developer.

The extension store at agentic-workstation-store.mzpizote.com is contacted only when you open the Store tab, install, update, rate or publish an extension. Browsing sends your search words and your IP address. To install, publish or review, the app signs the request with a key made on your computer and kept in your system keychain. The store keeps the public part of that key, the name you choose, the extensions you publish, which extensions that key installed, your ratings, reviews and reports. Published extensions, your name, ratings and reviews are public. The store does not keep IP addresses in its database.

Updates

Every four hours the app checks GitHub for a new version, and it downloads updates from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens.

What the app does not do

No analytics, no tracking, no crash reports and no ads. The developer does not sell your data and does not use it to train AI models.

This website

No cookies, no analytics and nothing loaded from other sites. The server keeps no access logs. Its error log, used only to fix problems, can include the IP address of a request that failed. The version number on the home page comes from GitHub through this server, so your browser contacts GitHub only when you download the app.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
